Comparison

ecom Tab-Ex 03 DZ1 vs
Bartec Agile X IS

Two ATEX and IECEx Zone 1 tablets, from ecom (Pepperl+Fuchs) and Bartec. Same zone, same gas group — but different design philosophies, operating systems, and enterprise ecosystems.

Quick Verdict

Choose Tab-Ex 03 DZ1 if:

  • • You prefer Android and mobile-first apps
  • • Samsung Knox ecosystem integration matters
  • • You want an AMOLED display and S Pen (Tab-Ex Pro DZ1)
  • • You want a lighter device (~690g vs ~1,050g)

Choose Bartec Agile X IS if:

  • • You need native Windows 10 in Zone 1
  • • SAP GUI, SCADA, or .NET apps are critical
  • • You already run Bartec phones (Pixavi) and want one supplier
  • • Full USB-A ports for peripherals are needed

Android vs Windows: Same Decision, Different Makers

Like the Tab-Ex vs Aegex comparison, this matchup fundamentally comes down to operating system. The Tab-Ex 03 DZ1 is an Android device built on Samsung Galaxy Tab hardware; the Bartec Agile X IS runs Windows 10 IoT Enterprise on Intel x86. The Agile X IS and the Aegex 10 IS are the two Zone 1 Windows tablets compared on this site.

What distinguishes this from the Aegex comparison is Bartec’s broader ecosystem: Bartec also makes the Pixavi smartphone and various Ex accessories. If your site already runs Bartec phones, choosing the Agile X means a single supplier for mobile devices, potentially simplifying procurement, spare parts, and support contracts.

Weight: A Significant Gap

At 690g vs 1,050g, the Tab-Ex is over 34% lighter. The Bartec Agile X IS is nearly as heavy as a small laptop, reflecting its x86 architecture, larger battery, and more robust chassis. For desk-bound or vehicle-mounted use this is irrelevant. For all-day carry in the field, it’s a genuine ergonomic consideration.

Where the Agile X IS Differs

The Agile X IS has a sunlight-readable display with Gorilla Glass; the Tab-Ex 03 DZ1 uses a TFT panel. For outdoor process areas where glare is a constant problem, compare both in a field trial. The Agile X also offers full USB-A ports, which simplifies connecting measurement instruments, label printers, or USB drives without adapters.

Bottom Line

Choose the Tab-Ex 03 DZ1 for Android-first, mobile-first workflows where weight and sealing matter: about 690 g, IP68 and a hot-swappable battery, against about 1,050 g and IP65 for the Agile X IS.

Choose the Bartec Agile X IS if Windows is a hard requirement and you value the sunlight-readable display, USB-A ports, and a single supplier for Bartec phones and tablets.
