Android vs Windows: Same Decision, Different Vendors

Like the Tab-Ex vs Aegex comparison, this matchup fundamentally comes down to operating system. The Tab-Ex 03 DZ2 is an Android device built on Samsung Galaxy Tab hardware; the Bartec Agile X IS runs Windows 10 IoT Enterprise on Intel x86.

What distinguishes this from the Aegex comparison is Bartec’s broader ecosystem: Bartec also makes the Pixavi smartphone and various Ex accessories. If your site already runs Bartec phones, choosing the Agile X means a single vendor for mobile devices, potentially simplifying procurement, spare parts, and support contracts.

Weight: A Significant Gap

At 690g vs 1,050g, the Tab-Ex is over 34% lighter. The Bartec Agile X IS is nearly as heavy as a small laptop, reflecting its x86 architecture, larger battery, and more robust chassis. For desk-bound or vehicle-mounted use this is irrelevant. For all-day carry in the field, it’s a genuine ergonomic consideration.

Where Bartec Genuinely Wins

The Agile X IS has a Gorilla Glass sunlight-readable display that outperforms the Tab-Ex in direct sunlight. For outdoor process areas where glare is a constant problem, this matters. The Agile X also offers full USB-A ports, which simplifies connecting measurement instruments, label printers, or USB drives without adapters.

Bartec’s service network is also particularly strong in European chemical and pharmaceutical sectors, where Bartec has deep account relationships.

Bottom Line

Choose the Tab-Ex 03 DZ2 for Android-first, mobile-first workflows where weight, battery life, and cost matter. It’s the lighter, more affordable, and more water-resistant option.

Choose the Bartec Agile X IS if Windows is a hard requirement and you value the sunlight display, USB-A ports, and Bartec’s single-vendor ecosystem for phones + tablets.
