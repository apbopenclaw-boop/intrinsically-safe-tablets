Comparison

Aegex 10 IS vs
ecom Tab-Ex 03 DZ1

The Aegex 10 IS (also written Aegex10) is a Windows ATEX tablet for Zone 1; the ecom Tab-Ex 03 DZ1 runs Android. Same zone rating, same gas group, different operating systems.

Quick Verdict

Choose Tab-Ex 03 DZ1 if:

  • • Your field apps run on Android (inspection, photo docs)
  • • Weight matters — 690g vs 850g is noticeable all day
  • • You want Samsung Knox enterprise management
  • • IP68 water/dust protection is required

Choose Aegex 10 IS if:

  • • You need native Windows (SAP GUI, SCADA, legacy .exe)
  • • Your IT runs Windows Endpoint Manager / SCCM
  • • Full desktop-class browser is essential
  • • You need USB-A peripherals (printers, instruments)

Operating System: The Defining Choice

This comparison comes down to one question: does your workflow require Windows?

The Aegex 10 IS runs full Windows 10 or 11, meaning it can execute legacy desktop software — SAP GUI, Pi ProcessBook, SCADA HMI clients, custom .NET inspection tools, and anything else compiled for x86. For refineries and chemical plants with decades of Windows-based process software, this is non-negotiable. An Android tablet cannot run these applications natively.

The Tab-Ex 03 DZ1 runs Android 13, which covers modern web-based inspection tools, cloud ERP, photo documentation, and barcode scanning. If your critical apps are browser-based or available on Google Play / managed via MDM, Android is sufficient, and the Tab-Ex 03 DZ1 is the lighter of the two devices. Samsung DeX also provides a desktop-like experience when docked.

Ingress Protection and Ruggedness

The Tab-Ex 03 DZ1 carries an IP68 rating (dust-tight, submersible beyond 1m), while the Aegex 10 IS is rated IP65 (dust-tight, water jets from any direction). In practice, IP68 provides better protection against accidental immersion — important when working around process water, rain, or cleaning operations. The Aegex can handle rain and splashes but should not be submerged.

Weight and Form Factor

At 690g vs 850g, the Tab-Ex is approximately 19% lighter. Over an 8-hour shift carrying a tablet between process units, climbing ladders, and working in confined spaces, 160g makes a real difference in hand fatigue. The Aegex is heavier partly because it houses an x86 processor and fan-less cooling architecture required for Windows workloads. Both are 10.1″ screens, so pocketability is equivalent.

Price and Total Cost of Ownership

Both tablets are priced on request. The unit price is only part of the total cost. If the Aegex removes the need for separate desktop terminals in control rooms or offices, its Windows capability may reduce total IT infrastructure cost. Android devices avoid per-device Windows licensing and generally need simpler MDM.

Where the Aegex 10 IS Fits

If Windows is a requirement, the Tab-Ex is not an option: it runs Android only. The Aegex 10 IS is one of two Zone 1 Windows tablets compared here; the other is the Bartec Agile X IS. The Getac F110-Ex also runs Windows, but it is certified for Zone 2 only.

Bottom Line

Choose the Tab-Ex 03 DZ1 if your apps are browser-based or Android-native and you want the lighter device (about 690 g) with IP68 sealing.

Choose the Aegex 10 IS if you have hard dependencies on Windows desktop software and need Zone 1 certification. It is heavier and rated IP65, and it runs SAP GUI or SCADA clients in a Zone 1 area without a separate fixed terminal. The Bartec Agile X IS is the other Zone 1 Windows tablet compared on this site.
