ecom Tab-Ex Alternatives
ATEX Zone 1 and Zone 2 Tablets Compared

The ecom Tab-Ex 03 DZ1 and Tab-Ex Pro DZ1 are ATEX and IECEx Zone 1 Android tablets built on Samsung Galaxy Tab Active hardware. Other certified tablets and iPad solutions suit different needs. This page lists them with zone, certificates, operating system and the main trade-offs.

Why Consider Tab-Ex Alternatives?

The Tab-Ex 03 DZ1 is a 10.1″ Zone 1 Android tablet, also covered in our best tablets guide. Common reasons to look at other devices:

  • You need Windows. The Tab-Ex is Android-only. If SAP GUI, SCADA clients, or legacy .exe apps are required, you need the Aegex 10 IS or Bartec Agile X IS.
  • You need a compact tablet. The Tab-Ex 03 DZ1 is 10.1″. The i.safe IS930.1 is 8″, which is easier to hold in one hand in the field.
  • You run iPadOS apps. Xshielder, Xciel and Atexxo offer certified cases or conversions for Apple iPads.
  • Zone 2 is sufficient. If your area classification only requires Zone 2, the Getac F110-Ex (Windows 11) or Zebra XPAD L10 IS (Android or Windows) are options.
  • Supplier diversification. Some procurement policies require more than one supplier to avoid dependency on a single manufacturer.

i.safe MOBILE IS930.1 Zone 1ATEXIECEx

Compact Zone 1 Android. The IS930.1 has an 8″ IPS screen and weighs about 570 g, against 10.1″ and about 690 g for the Tab-Ex 03 DZ1. Some field workers prefer the smaller size for one-handed use. It runs Android 12 (the Tab-Ex 03 DZ1 runs Android 13). Gas Group IIC, IP68, ATEX and IECEx.

Aegex 10 IS Zone 1ATEXIECEx

A Zone 1 Windows tablet. If your field operations depend on SAP GUI, PI ProcessBook, SCADA HMI, or other Windows applications, the Aegex 10 IS runs them natively in Zone 1. It is one of two Zone 1 Windows tablets compared here (Aegex 10 IS, Bartec Agile X IS). Compared with the Tab-Ex 03 DZ1 it is heavier (about 850 g) and rated IP65 instead of IP68. Android has no workaround for .exe dependencies.

Quick Decision Guide

Need Zone 1 + Android? → Tab-Ex 03 DZ1 or Tab-Ex Pro DZ1 (10.1″) or i.safe IS930.1 (8″)

Need Zone 1 + Windows? → Aegex 10 IS or Bartec Agile X IS

Zone 2 is enough + want large screen? → Getac F110-Ex

Zone 2 + Zebra enterprise ecosystem? → Zebra XPAD L10 IS

Compact device for Zone 1? → i.safe IS930.1 (8″ Android) or Xciel XCZ1 iPad mini (8.3″ iPadOS)

Apple ecosystem in Zone 1? → Xshielder iPad Pro 11 (11″, ATEX) or Xciel XCZ1 iPad mini (compact, ATEX and IECEx); Atexxo’s Zone 1 converted iPad 11″ is announced

Apple ecosystem, Zone 2 is enough? → Atexxo converted iPad (A16) or Xciel XCRiPad cases
